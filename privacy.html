Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
This policy explains what information LabourArc collects, how it's used, and the rights you have over it. LabourArc is a workforce management and payroll tool used by employers ("Employers") to track hours, job sites, and pay for their crew members ("Employees").

1. Information We Collect

From Employers (account holders)

  • Name and email address (used to create and secure your login)
  • Company name, address, and contact details entered into your Company Profile
  • A PIN you set for accessing the app

From Employees (added by an Employer)

  • Name, hourly rate, and an optional PIN set by the Employer or Employee
  • Optional contact details (phone, email), hire date, and certifications, if entered
  • Hours worked, job sites, clock-in/out timestamps, and any notes attached to timesheet entries
  • Push notification subscription tokens, if notifications are enabled

Automatically Collected

  • Basic device and usage information needed to operate the app (e.g. browser type, general error logs)

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate LabourArc's core functionality:

  • Recording and calculating hours worked, overtime, deductions, and pay
  • Generating payslips and payroll summaries
  • Sending payroll summaries and notifications by email or push, where enabled
  • Allowing Employers to manage their crew and job sites

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How Information Is Stored and Shared

Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider. Emails are sent through Resend, a third-party email delivery service. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for their own purposes.

Within a single company's account (workspace), Employers can see the timesheet, contact, and pay information of their own Employees. Data is not shared across different companies using LabourArc.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained for as long as an Employer's account is active. Employers can permanently delete their account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes the company's workspace, all Employee records, timesheets, and payroll history, and cannot be undone.

Employers are responsible for retaining their own copies of payroll records as required by applicable employment and tax law before deleting their account.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. You can exercise most of these directly in the app (editing your profile, exporting a backup, or deleting your account), or by contacting us using the details below.

6. Canadian Users (PIPEDA)

For users in Canada, we handle personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). This means we collect only the information reasonably needed to provide the service, obtain consent through your use of the app and acceptance of these policies, and give you the ability to access and request deletion of your information as described above.

7. U.S. State Privacy Laws

For users in states with comprehensive privacy laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws in states including Virginia, Colorado, and Connecticut), you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of certain uses. LabourArc does not sell personal information and does not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Requests can be made using the contact details below.

8. Children's Privacy

LabourArc is a workplace tool intended for use by adults in an employment context and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 16.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect information stored in LabourArc. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
